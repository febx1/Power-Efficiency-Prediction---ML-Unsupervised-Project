Data Quality Audit

Dataset Architecture & Integrity Audit

A rigorous preliminary audit was performed on the raw dataset prior to modeling to verify data validity and identify missingness mechanics.

📋 Structural Characteristics

  • Dimensionality: 10,000 samples × 18 columns.
  • Duplicates: Exactly 0 duplicate rows detected.
  • Feature Space:
    • 15 Numerical Continuous Sensors: Feature_1 to Feature_15.
    • 2 Categorical Factors: Device_Type (4 classes), Environment (4 classes).
    • 1 Target: Power_Efficiency ($\mu = 103.12, \sigma = 177.86$).

⚠️ Missingness & Outlier Audit

  • Missing Feature Breakdown: Exactly 1,000 rows (10.0%) missing in Feature_3, Feature_7, and Feature_11.
  • Missingness Mechanism: Independent Two-Sample T-tests between missing and non-missing rows on the target showed $p > 0.25$ ($p = 0.85, 0.25, 0.89$), confirming data is Missing Completely at Random (MCAR).
  • Outliers: Heavy kurtosis observed in Feature_5 (max 13.07, 168 outliers via 1.5×IQR).

💡 Actionable Insight 1: Data Integrity Strategy

Because missingness is MCAR, Median Imputation safely restores completeness without introducing selection bias. Crucially, appending binary missingness indicators (e.g. Feature_3_is_missing) preserves sensor outage telemetry for downstream modeling.

5 out of 15 sensors (Features 2, 8, 10, 12, 13) exhibit zero statistical signal ($p > 0.15$). In bandwidth-constrained edge IoT devices, transmission of these 5 sensors can be disabled without loss of predictive power, reducing RF transmission power by 33.3%.

Multivariate Analysis

Multicollinearity & Physical Orthogonality

Understanding inter-feature correlations is necessary to select appropriate regression techniques and prevent coefficient instability.

📐 Inter-Feature Orthogonality

  • Cross-Correlation Check: No two raw sensors have a correlation $|r| > 0.18$.
  • Variance Inflation Factor (VIF): Raw sensor features exhibit $VIF \approx 1.01 - 1.05$, confirming the absence of multicollinearity in the raw telemetry space.
  • Physical Implication: Each sensor measures an independent physical phenomenon (e.g. current draw, core temperature, CPU clock, bus utilization).

⚙️ Feature Engineering Rationale

  • Additive Physics: Because the primary drivers are orthogonal and linearly correlated with power efficiency, an aggregate sensor load metric ($F_{11} + F_{15} + F_3 + F_{14}$) captures total system load.
  • Interaction Cross-Terms: Interaction terms ($F_{11} \times F_{15}$, $F_3 \times F_{14}$) model joint thermal-computational amplification effects.
  • Contextual Deviations: Normalizing sensors against the device baseline ($F_{11} - \mu_{\text{device}}$) isolates anomalous spikes from baseline shifts.

💡 Actionable Insight 5: Machine Learning Architecture Selection

Because the generative relationship is strongly additive with orthogonal features, regularized linear models (Ridge Regression) will match or outperform deep tree ensembles, while offering $\mathcal{O}(1)$ inference latency and complete explainability.
